What each button does

Inbox (a recording waiting for your OK)

▶ / AI notes
Transcribe it on the M14, then the AI writes the summary, tasks and decisions. Shows the time and cost first.
Transcript only
Transcribed on the M14, free. Make AI notes on the talk later if you want them.
⏱ Process at…
Pick a time (or Tonight at 1 am) and AI notes or Transcript only: it waits, then starts by itself. The row shows ⏱ and the time; Cancel that in its sheet undoes it.
Remind me
A reminder only: it hides the recording from Needs you until the time you pick. Nothing is processed; you still decide.
Delete
Moves it to Trash (bottom of Talks). Restore it there within 30 days; after that it and its audio are deleted for good.
Read / + Transcript
Read a phone transcript that synced with it, or paste one in.
Select
Long-press a row (or Select). The top shows how many and Select all; the bottom bar has AI notes, Transcript, Merge (one recording, in recorded order; Unmerge works until it's processed) and Delete. ✕ or a tap anywhere else stops selecting.

While something is processing

❚❚ Pause
Stops at the next safe point (within about 30 s) so the computer is free. Nothing done so far is lost.
▶ Resume now · ☾ Resume at 1 am
Carries on from where it stopped, now or tonight.
✕ Cancel
Only while paused: back to the Inbox as it was; the half-done files are deleted.
Processing history
⋯ at the top › Processing history: Pause / Resume at the top, then everything you sent, when, how long it took, AI or not.
Speed (Processing history)
Auto (normal): full speed while nobody uses the Mac; half the cores while someone does (keyboard or mouse, SSH, Screen Sharing, RustDesk, or a Claude / Codex chat while it runs heavy work), back to full 10 minutes after. Every night 1 to 7 AM: the Inbox is worked through oldest first as Transcript only (no AI, no cost), one after another; recordings with a Process at time wait for it. Make AI notes on any of them later. Full: always all 4 cores. Gentle: always half, about twice as long, cooler. Night only: new recordings wait until 1 AM.
🌡️ Hot Mac
At 95 °C for 5 minutes processing pauses by itself (Discord says so) and carries on once the Mac has been under 80 °C for 5 minutes. After 2 hours it carries on with half the cores.
From Discord
The pinned M14 board in #m14-health has ❚❚ Pause processing and ▶ Resume buttons.

A talk

A dotted name
Not named yet. Tap it to say who it is.
Tap a coloured name
Change who said those lines. ✂ Only part of it? then tap the first word the other person said. ✎ Fix words edits the text.
Tap a line
Plays the audio from there.
The right edge
The page outline: tap or drag to jump to a section. The first row (↑ and the page name) goes back to the top. On People, letter groups open into single letters while you're in them.
Short | Detailed
Tabs next to Summary. Detailed is a longer version by topic, made only when you ask (it shows the cost first).

Tasks and People

The circle
Done.
Tap or long-press a task
Its whole title, then one row each for Due, Who, Remind me and the talk (tap a row to change just that), then Done, ✎ Edit and ⋯ (Not needed, Remove).
People › Select
The initials circles become round checkboxes; then Merge (same person, keep one name) or Remove (Undo for 10 minutes).
Voices not named yet
The row at the top of People: speakers who were given tasks or notes before they had a name. Pick who it is; the talk, Tasks and People all update.

Fixing mistakes

Remove a wrong task, decision or note from its ⋯; an Undo bar appears. Change person moves it to the right one.

Mis-heard words: ✎ Fix words; Also teach the glossary fixes it in future AI runs.

Nothing just disappears: Trash keeps a talk or a deleted recording 30 days with Restore, then deletes it and its audio; dismissed items stay in History with Restore; Snoozed items come back at their time.

Privacy, money and the computer

Audio never leaves the M14. Only transcript text goes to the AI, and only when you approve. Anything that costs money shows the price first; there's a monthly cap (Settings › AI & processing).

Processing runs at low priority, so the app stays quick; ❚❚ Pause frees the computer completely.
