{{ m.section("Fixing what the AI got wrong") }}

Secondary actions live under the ⋯ button on each row, so screens stay clean.

Wrong, remove: removes a task, decision or note. An Undo bar appears for a few seconds.

Change person: moves a task or note to the right person. Moving a task to or from you switches it between You owe and They owe you.

Speaker names: tap a name under the conversation title to rename that speaker everywhere, or use the ⋯ on one transcript line to change just that line's speaker or words. When fixing a mis-heard word, Also teach the glossary makes future AI runs fix it automatically.

Merge: on a person's page, use Merge into another person when two entries are the same person.

Nothing just disappears: Move to Trash (a conversation's ⋯) keeps it, with its tasks and notes, for 30 days under Trash (at the bottom of Conversations), where Restore brings it back; after that it's removed for good with its tasks. Dismissed Needs you items stay in History with Restore.

Every fix counts toward Scribe Bot's quality score, and a named voice is recognised next time.

{{ m.section("Privacy and money") }}

Audio never leaves the M14. Only transcript text goes to the AI, and only when you approve it. Transcript Only (No AI) keeps a recording fully private.

Anything that costs money asks you to confirm first, with the price. There's a hard monthly cap (Settings › AI & processing).
