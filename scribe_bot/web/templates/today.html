How to use Scribe Bot

  1. Record in Google Recorder as usual. It syncs to the M14 in seconds.
  2. Approve it in the Inbox (the tab next to Today): Summarize for a summary and tasks, or Transcript only (free, stays on the M14).
  3. Read it in Talks when the "Ready" ping arrives. The strip at the top says what to check.
  4. Fix anything wrong right there: tap Done, or ⋯ for Change person, Edit and Remove. Tap a speaker's name to rename them. Every change can be undone.
  5. Find it later in Talks, People, Tasks (tasks and decisions) and Search.
