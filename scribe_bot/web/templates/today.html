How to use Scribe Bot

  1. Record in Google Recorder as usual. It syncs to the M14 in seconds.
  2. Approve it under Needs you, right here on Today: Transcribe + AI for a summary and tasks, or Transcript Only (No AI) to keep it fully private.
  3. Read it in Conversations when the "Ready" ping arrives. The strip at the top says what to check.
  4. Fix anything wrong right there: tap Done, or ⋯ for Change person, Edit and Remove. Tap a speaker's name to rename them. Every change can be undone.
  5. Find it later in Talks (conversations and people), Tasks (tasks and decisions) and Search.

Corrections teach Scribe Bot: a named voice is recognised next time, and fixes count toward its quality score.
